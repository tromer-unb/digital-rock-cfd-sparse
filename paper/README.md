# Display items of the paper

The figure files and Supplementary Tables exactly as used in the article. They are the **targets** of the reproduction:
`reproduce/02_figures_from_results.py` and `reproduce/03_figures_and_tables_as_run.py` regenerate them from `data/` and
compare the result with these files.

The file names follow the order in which the figures were made, not their final numbers in the article:

|---|---|---|---|
| `figures/figure1.pdf` | Figure 1 | samples and percolating domains | step 3 |
| `figures/figure3.pdf` | Figure 2 | sensitivity to inlet condition, reservoir width and contour tolerance | step 3 |
| `figures/figure5.pdf` | Figure 3 | verification and validation (Poiseuille, cylinder array, OpenFOAM) | step 3 |
| `figures/figure2.pdf` | Figure 4 | mesh convergence (Taylor–Hood and Mini) | step 2 |
| `figures/figure4.pdf` | Figure 5 | cropping window; pixel finite volume vs contour finite element | step 3 |
| `figures/figure6.pdf` | Figure 6 | unknowns, solve time and memory of LU and MINRES | step 3 |
| `figures/figure7.pdf` | Figure 7 | MINRES and Mini accuracy; matched sub-window timing | step 3 |
| `figures/figure8.pdf` | Figure 8 | pressure and velocity fields | step 2 |

Supplementary Tables 1 (symbols), 5 (first-release pipeline on Poiseuille channels) and 8 (mesh levels) are typed in the
Supplementary Information itself; the data of Table 5 are in `data/results/legacy_poiseuille_rerun/`.

Step 2 compares the numbers plotted (`*_source.json`, identical to `data/results/figure_sources/`); step 3 compares the
tables byte for byte and the figures on the page rendered at 200 dpi (the PDF writer stores a creation date, so PDF bytes
differ between runs).
